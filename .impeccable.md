## Design Context

### Users

AI-assisted builders and their coding agents use Stack Blueprint at the beginning of a software project. They want a decisive, current application stack and a fully equipped AI workspace without researching dozens of skills, MCPs, CLIs, and product integrations themselves.

### Brand Personality

Judgmental, precise, constructive. The experience should create confidence and curiosity: the system quietly does substantial research, then reveals a legible plan that feels engineered rather than generated.

### Aesthetic Direction

An architect's drafting desk and issued construction drawing set. Technical lettering, measured rules, sheet identifiers, revision marks, plan symbols, and staged drawing motion. It must not resemble Arjay's large-sans stack picker, a selectable logo catalog, a generic SaaS dashboard, or retro blueprint cosplay.

### Design Principles

1. Lead with the equipped AI workspace; the conventional application stack supports it.
2. Show the answer as a connected drawing, with rationale and provenance available on demand.
3. Every line, label, and animation communicates structure, state, or sequence.
4. Prefer exact named tools and project instructions over generic categories or article summaries.
5. Keep the first view calm: no alternatives grid, no dense evidence dump, and no choices the system can make itself.
