# Agent Blueprint — Design Contract

## Product context

Agent Blueprint is the first conversation at the start of an agent project. A builder and browser agent progressively choose the model strategy, runtime, tools, skills, memory, evaluation system, and execution boundary. The memorable thing is: **the architecture is revealed one decision at a time, and the human owns every lock.**

Audience: AI-assisted builders and agent engineers who need informed starting decisions without pretending there is one universal stack.

## Aesthetic direction

An active architectural drafting table, not a SaaS landing page and not retro blueprint cosplay. Dense, instructional, precise, and visibly under construction. Blueprint plans and material schedules inform the structure; no source is copied visually.

Decoration level: low. Every line denotes structure, state, measurement, or hierarchy.

## Typography

- Barlow Condensed: upright display, section titles, decision names, and the wordmark.
- IBM Plex Mono: labels, controls, annotations, measurements, and explanatory copy.
- Tabular numerals everywhere. Uppercase tracking only for labels.
- No italic wordmark, serif display face, system font, or oversized marketing headline.

## Color

- Drawing field: deep blueprint navy.
- Primary linework: desaturated cyan.
- Reading surface: pale vellum.
- Ink: near-black blue.
- One signal color: survey yellow.

Survey yellow is reserved for the primary kickoff action, the selected option, locked-decision hardware, and progress. It is never decorative.

## Content encoding

- Each layer must show three structurally different approaches, not vendor-logo collections.
- Every option must expose `best for` and `tradeoff` before selection.
- Agent recommendations may name a specific implementation and cite only evidence searched through the page.
- A recommendation never silently becomes a decision. Only an explicit lock advances the drawing.
- No transcripts, article bodies, private data, vanity inventory totals, or invented performance scores.

## Layout

- Working-instrument composition: masthead, compact project brief, ruled architecture drawing, field desk, build sheet.
- Layers share a fixed rail/body anatomy and reveal vertically.
- The evidence field desk is secondary and sticky on wide screens; it stacks below decisions on mobile.
- Corners are square. Borders are structural. No card shadows, bubbles, or ornamental containers.

## Motion

- Newly revealed layers draw in with opacity and a short vertical translation under 240ms.
- Locking uses color and line-weight state change, not bounce or celebration.
- Never animate text while it is being read.
- Reduced motion preserves the reveal hierarchy with near-instant transitions.

## Mobile

- One column, no horizontal scrolling.
- Option grids stack; every action is at least 44px high.
- Field desk follows the active layer rather than competing beside it.
- Labels may abbreviate, but tradeoffs never disappear.

## Anti-patterns

No gradients, rounded card grids, decorative icons, blobs, grain, faux 3D, generic hero copy, centered-everything layouts, duplicate calls to action, or screenshot-shaped fake previews.
